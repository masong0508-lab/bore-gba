# BORE: GODGUY (Nintendo DS)

A god game spun off from BORE: shape a 128 x 128 world with the stylus, drop in life, and watch it live, eat, hunt and breed.

**v0.5: ENEMIES, STORY, SAVES, BIG PLANETS, 82 TRACKS.**
- **320 monsters** (20 kinds x 16 colours, from imps and ghouls to krakens, liches and the elder dragon; four new sprite shapes). They rise from rifts far from the villages, hunt people first, burn houses and are shot by towers; stronger kinds come as the world ages and as you finish chapters. Villagers fight back, and gifts of weapons and armour decide who wins.
- **Story**: 14 chapters (use your powers, found a people, build homes, meet and slay monsters, give gifts, grow a hall, reach years...). Each chapter you finish opens a tier of items for good, so free play only has what the story has earned (L+R+START opens everything). Page 1 of the top screen (SELECT) shows the chapter; page 2 is the **bestiary** (320 entries, ? until met, * when slain).
- **Saving**: the whole world, villages, bestiary and story go to `godguy.sav` on the SD card (libfat) every year; L+R+SELECT saves now; the title screen continues it. Tested on the PC: a loaded game runs on exactly like the unsaved one (all three planet sizes), and damaged or truncated files are refused.
- **Planets of 128, 192 and 256 tiles a side** (Y on the title screen); the 3D globe, minimap and picking scale to any size.
- **Music: 82 tracks and 29 sound effects**, amalgamated from the visible songs of BORE (tools/gm_analyze.py reads the .xm files for each song's tempo, key, mode and repeated hooks; tools/gm2_compose.py re-keys, re-modes and re-times them). 38 tracks are for places and moments (title, each biome, time of day, wars, each boss, chapter jingles, credits), 32 are full songs of 3-6 minutes (intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, breakdown, second verse, key-lift chorus, a bridge in the opposite mode, final chorus, outro) and 12 are long minimalist pieces of 3-10 minutes (cycling arpeggios, additive process, phasing, 3 against 4). The engine plays 10 looped wave voices (lead, ping-pong echo, counter, harmony, two detuned pads, bass, arpeggio, sparkle, and a Haas double of the lead), a synthesised sampled drum kit on four slots and the effects on a PSG pulse and the noise channel, in stereo: tested on the PC for clipping (none), left/right balance (mean 0.06) and width. Page 3 of the top screen is a **jukebox**.
- Keys: SELECT page, X+Y toggles the 3D globe, SELECT+START toggles story / free play (new world), START new world.
- Tests: `tools/run_tests.sh` (world, 3D picking, saving, story bot at three sizes, music render). The DS build itself is NOT verified in an emulator.

**v0.4: THE 3D PLANET IS PLAYABLE.** In 3D view the planet now sits on the touch screen and the stylus works on it directly: touch the globe to use the chosen power (paint, spawn, rain, fire, smite, gift, inspect, ...) on the exact tile under the pen, and drag to paint. Hold B and drag to spin the planet by hand; the D-pad still turns it, X / Y zoom, A changes shape, L / R change power. The pen is turned into a map tile by projecting the mesh exactly as the GPU does and picking the nearest front surface, so it works on the sphere, cube world and ring world. `gcc -O2 -DPCTEST -o pick_test pick_test.c -lm && ./pick_test` round-trips tiles through screen and back (not-grazing tiles come back within 1-4 tiles; on the ring world a tile hidden behind the near side of the ring returns what you see instead). Untested on real hardware or an emulator: no available emulator boots this build.

**v0.3: ITEMS, 3D PLANETS, LIGHT.** SELECT toggles a 3D planet view on the top screen (the touch screen keeps the HUD and map): D-pad turns the planet, X / Y zoom from far out to skimming the surface, A cycles the shape: SPHERE, CUBE WORLD, RING WORLD (the 128 x 128 map wrapped on each; it is re-painted every second). A 364-item catalogue (14 materials x 8 weapons, x 6 armours, 20 potions, seeds, foods, 40 relics, gems, god tools x 4 grades) is generated from tables; the GIFT power hands the picked item (X / Y cycle, with B held jumps ten) to a creature, a village or the land. Better things unlock as the world ages (years, folk, homes, level 3 halls); START + SELECT = free play, everything open. The 2D world now has a day and night cycle and shimmering water. PC previews: `gcc -O2 -DPCTEST -o pc3d pc3d.c -lm` draws the three planets.

**v0.2: VILLAGES.** Each people founds villages (a person with food and no home within 14 tiles settles). Villagers gather into a stockpile, eat from it, breed faster at home and stay close; villages build huts, houses, farms, towers and bigger halls (3 levels), raze to ruins if they die out, burn under fire / lightning / meteors. The orcs, goblins and ogres go to war with a neighbouring people; armies march, fight and raid. New powers: SETTLE, WAR, PEACE, BLESS; INSPECT on a building names the village.

**v0.1:** world generator (continents, oceans, ice caps, deserts, jungles, swamps, lava), 12 ground types as generated tiles, 392 creature kinds (8 peoples + 24 animal archetypes x 16 colour families, every one with its own name, shape, diet, habitat and speed), a living ecosystem (grazing, hunting, breeding, starvation, old age, dirt regrowing into grass), and 21 divine powers (paint the ground, spawn, rain, fire, lightning, meteor, smite, heal, feed, inspect).

Controls: stylus = use the power; D-pad = pan (B faster); L / R = power; X / Y = brush size, or species while SPAWN is picked; SELECT = +10 species; A = pause; START = new world. Top screen: HUD and the whole-planet map.

Build: `make` with devkitARM + libnds. PC test of the world and the ecosystem: `gcc -O2 -DPCTEST -o pc pc.c && ./pc <seed> <years>` (prints populations, writes /tmp/gg_world.ppm and /tmp/gg_zoom.ppm).

Roadmap: villages and civilisations that build, wars, disasters over time, 300+ items and tools, 300+ enemies, the story mode that unlocks free-play items, bigger planets (the world arrays are the limit: 256 x 256 fits the DS's 4 MB), saves, then PSP / Vita / 3DS ports.
