## BORE (GBA, FULL GAME)  https://dippertronics.itch.io/bore-gba-full-game
Check first: the download is `bore-base.gba` (27 MB). That is the base game WITHOUT the expansion packs. The complete game (all packs built in) is `bore.gba` (about 28.7 MB). Upload that one as the main file.

Short description (tagline): A voxel life sim for the Game Boy Advance: make a Bore, build a house, skate the town, fall in love, start a fight, grow something green.

Tags (10): Game Boy Advance, Life Simulation, Isometric, Voxel, Character Customization, Skating, Sandbox, Comedy, Retro, Adult. (Genre: Simulation. Platform note: it is a GBA ROM, so add an emulator line in the description.)

Description:

**BORE is a life sim that fits in a Game Boy Advance cartridge.** Make a creature with hundreds of sliders, move into a house on a street full of neighbours, and live. Everything is drawn in code: voxel people, an isometric town, a skate park, a bar, an arcade, a pool.

**What you do**
- **Make your Bore.** A full creature creator: body, face, hair, clothes, tails, horns, voice, personality, aspirations. Share codes let you hand a Bore to a friend.
- **Live in a town.** Pick a lot, build and furnish (hundreds of items), visit shops, gyms, a taco place, a casino, a cinema, a skate shop, a hospital. Neighbours become friends, rivals or partners.
- **Skate.** Ramps, rails, combos, a board you have to find or buy.
- **Let them live.** Your Sim eats, sleeps, chats and cheers itself up when you put the pad down. Housemates have free will and look after each other when they are glum.
- **Stories.** Twelve story modes, including TV SHOW & TELL: a fallen TV judge wins her fans back in five chapters, with cutscenes and an original song.
- **Expansion packs, built in.** BORE-OUT MAN! (a space oddity), BOREZIES! (the bored walk among us, plus horde mode), WORLD WAR BORE (a war over a hedge), BORTUA FIGHTER (a fighting game with a 2D arcade mode and a real-time 3D BALLZ mode), BOREAGOTCHI! (a handheld pet and real pets), BOREPILOGUE (grown-ups only: retire and leave a legacy), BOREFORE TIME (be a giant in a land of tiny folk).

**Good to know**
- Grown-up humour, drug references and an adults-only nudity option (off for under-age characters).
- Saves to the cartridge. Works in mGBA, other GBA emulators and on flash carts.
- Controls: D-pad to move, A / B, L / R, START for the menu. Every option is in the in-game OPTIONS.

Screenshots to upload (in this order): the new creator title (`docs/cover.png`), a house interior, the town map, a skate shot, TV SHOW & TELL cutscene, BORTUA FIGHTER BALLZ.

## Tag and listing tips
- itch gives you 10 tags per game; the current pages use 2 to 8. Fill them all.
- Add a line like "Needs a GBA emulator (mGBA) or a flash cart" at the very top of each description, because ROM downloads confuse people.
- Add 3 to 5 screenshots and one GIF per game; the first screenshot is the thumbnail in search.
- Set "Updated" devlogs: one short post per release ("TV SHOW & TELL has a new house, a villain and flashbacks") brings people back.
